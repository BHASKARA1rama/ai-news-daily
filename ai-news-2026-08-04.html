Delhi High Court rules in landmark ANI v OpenAI copyright case

The Delhi High Court delivered India's first major ruling on AI and copyright, handing OpenAI a double win. The court held that ChatGPT did not reproduce enough of news agency ANI's articles to constitute infringement, and that training on the articles likely falls within copyright exemptions.

The ruling exposes how hard it is for rights holders to prove their work was used in model training. The court noted that ANI manipulated ChatGPT using adversarial prompting to extract verbatim quotes, which weakened its case.
